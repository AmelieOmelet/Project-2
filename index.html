Background Story

Echoes Of The Drift Belt

In 2189, the Helios Relay around Neptune went silent after a chain of mining detonations ripped through the Drift Belt. You are Commander Kim, the last pilot cleared for deep-void recovery, flying a patched Orbital frame with twin missile launchers and a hull held together by luck.

Every asteroid you break reveals encrypted fragments from the lost relay. Reach each new thousand points and your weapons surge into frenzy protocol for ten seconds, burning through debris while the signal locks in. The relay logs also describe a forbidden time-travel drive called the Chrono Anchor, a prototype that can rewind local reality by a few moments. Survive long enough and you might recover the final transmission, jump backward through the blast, and learn who sabotaged the belt before it ever happened.
