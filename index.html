Background Story

The Reader Who Stayed Behind

The sky did not end all at once. It opened like a page turning, and the world was forced into a story only a single reader recognized. You are Kim, the one person who finished the final chapter before the disaster arrived, the only witness who knew the rules, the enemies, and the price of every choice.

Strange constellations now watch from above, each one an audience member whispering conditions, rewards, and punishments into the dark. Every asteroid you destroy is another scenario cleared, another fragment of the narrative forced back into place. Push past each thousand-point threshold and your firepower enters a frenzy state, as if the world itself is rewarding you for refusing to become a background character. Survive long enough, and you may reach the hidden ending that was never meant to be read.
