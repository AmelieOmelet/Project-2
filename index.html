Background Story

Last Survivor Of The Scenario

The sky split open without warning, and the planet was swallowed by a system of trials written into the stars. You are Kim, the only survivor who remembers how the world looked before the first scenario began, and the only pilot who understands what the constellations really want.

Every asteroid is another test, every wave another chance to prove you belong in the story and not beneath it. Clear the field, collect shields, and survive long enough to reach frenzy mode as the system pushes back harder. If you can keep moving, you might uncover the truth behind the collapse and force the scenario to regress before it claims everything left in the dark.
